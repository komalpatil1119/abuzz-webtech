OSM case study

Client :

Yashwantrao Chavan College (YCC), Nagpur – Autonomous College

Challenge :

YCC struggled with slow manual evaluation, heavy answer sheet bundles, no tracking of evaluators, and delayed results. Cutting answer sheets for scanning was strictly not allowed as per examination regulations.

Solution by Abuzz Webtech
  • Implemented non-cutting OSM machine to scan bound answer booklets without tearing
  • Introduced digital evaluation portal for examiners
  • Automated student-paper mapping through barcodes
  • Enabled real-time monitoring for COE
  • Added digital grievance & re-evaluation module with auto-refund
Outcome
  • 80% faster evaluation
  • 100% compliance with exam regulations
  • Zero manual calculation errors
  • Complete transparency with real-time dashboards
  • Digital archive ready for NAAC, NBA, and NIRF
Conclusion

Abuzz Webtech’s OSM transformed YCC’s evaluation into a fast, compliant, and fully digital process, proving its effectiveness for large autonomous institutions.
