What is OSM?

⚙️ Challenges in Traditional Evaluation

Despite technological progress, most educational institutions still face major issues in examination evaluation.

Traditional manual and semi-digital systems often fail to ensure security, compliance, and efficiency.

However, as per university and examination board regulations, no physical damage or alteration to an answer booklet is allowed once it is sealed and submitted by the student.

✂️ Tearing or Cutting During Scanning (Regulation Violation)

Traditional OSM systems require answer sheets to be torn, unbound, or cut before scanning which violates audit and compliance norms, risks loss or misplacement of pages, and can raise legal or accreditation issues during inspections.

⚖️ Non-Compliance with Accreditation Requirements

Manual processes make it difficult to generate NAAC, NBA, or NIRF-aligned reports and maintain proper audit trails.

📦 Heavy Bundles & Dusty Storage

Managing thousands of physical booklets creates logistical, hygiene, and record-keeping challenges for colleges and universities.

♂️ High Manpower Dependency

Multiple staff are needed for sorting, labeling, verifying, and distributing answer sheets manually increasing cost and complexity.

🧾 Manual Checking & Human Error

Paper-based checking consumes excessive time and often leads to marking inconsistencies and missing entries.

🔒 Lack of Transparency & Tracking

It’s difficult to monitor evaluation progress, trace which examiner checked which paper, or generate real-time reports.

🕐 Delayed Result Declaration

Physical handling, transportation, and cross-verification of answer sheets significantly delay result announcements.

📄 Re-Evaluation Complexity

Physical rechecking requires handling fragile papers again, making it slow and error-prone.
