What is Learning Management System Software?

A Learning Management System Software (LMS) is a digital education platform designed to create, manage, deliver, and track online learning activities for educational institutions and training organizations.

It enables institutions to manage:

  • Deliver online courses and training programs.
  • Manage student learning progress.
  • Conduct online assessments and examinations.
  • Share educational content digitally.
  • Automate academic workflows.
