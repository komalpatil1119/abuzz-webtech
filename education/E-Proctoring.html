Key Features

AI-Based Candidate Authentication

Live face verification using webcam, Photo/ID matching, Auto-flagging of impersonation, Multi-step verification for high-stakes exams.

Real-Time Monitoring Dashboard

Monitor hundreds of candidates at once, Live candidate video feed, Audio monitoring and background noise detection, Automatic alerts for suspicious behavior.

Automated Cheating Detection

Our AI flags violations such as:

Mptiple faces detected, Candidate leaving the screen, Use of mobile devices, Unusual body movements, External conversations, Screen switching or tab changing.

Secure Browser Lock

Restricts opening new tabs, Blocks copy/paste, Prevents screen sharing, Disables external applications.

Auto-Flag & Report Generation

Detailed report for each candidate, Time-stamped screenshots, Violation score & summary, Downloadable report for COE/Exam Cell.

Exam Recording & Playback

Complete recording of video, audio, and screen, Easily accessible for audits or dispute resolution.

Seamless ERP Integration

Auto-sync with Student Master, Exam Timetable, and Hall Tickets, Reports directly accessible to COE, HODs, and Admins, No extra login required.

Multi-Device Support

Students can attend exams from laptop, desktop, or institution-approved tablets. Automatic detection and blocking of unauthorized secondary devices. Optimized performance even on low-bandwidth networks.

Automated Environment Scanning

360° room scan using webcam before exam starts. Detects presence of books, notes, or external persons. AI verifies whether the exam environment is compliant and secure.
