What is College Management System Software?

A College Management System Software is an integrated ERP platform designed to automate and manage all academic and administrative processes of colleges.

It helps institutions manage:

  • Admissions and student lifecycle.
  • Academic planning and curriculum.
  • Examination and evaluation systems.
  • Attendance and course management.
  • Finance, payroll, and HR.
  • Compliance (NAAC, NEP 2020, OBE).

Why Colleges Need ERP Software
