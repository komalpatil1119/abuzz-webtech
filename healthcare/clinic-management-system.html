Faculty Performance Evaluation

The PMS allows for regular assessment of faculty performance based on teaching effectiveness, research contributions, and student feedback.

Evaluations help in identifying areas of strength and opportunities for professional development.

Student Performance Tracking

Continuously tracks student progress across courses, assignments, exams, and extracurricular activities, allowing for an in-depth understanding of academic performance and engagement.

The process of performance tracking also fosters collaboration between teachers and parents, promoting a holistic approach to student development.

Goal Setting and Monitoring

Facilitates the setting of performance goals for both faculty and students, ensuring alignment with academic standards and personal growth objectives

The system monitors progress toward these goals, providing feedback along the way.

360-Degree Feedback

Includes feedback from multiple sources, such as students, peers, and administrators, to give a holistic view of performance.

The idea is to collect a broad spectrum of insights, which helps the individual receiving the feedback to understand how they are perceived in various contexts and from different perspectives.

Customizable Evaluation Metrics

Allows customization of performance metrics based on institutional goals and individual expectations.

This includes course delivery quality, research output, student engagement, and overall effectiveness.

Real-Time Performance Analytics

Provides real-time analytics to track performance trends, identify patterns, and provide immediate feedback.

This data-driven approach helps both faculty and students understand their strengths and areas for improvement.

Performance Reviews and Appraisals

Automates the performance review process for faculty, with scheduled appraisals that assess progress over a given period.

This can be linked to promotions, salary increments, or professional development opportunities.

Continuous Feedback Mechanism

Encourages regular, ongoing feedback, rather than just annual or semester-based reviews.

This helps address issues early and makes continuous improvements in performance.
