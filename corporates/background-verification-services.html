Identity Verification

Identity verification is the process of confirming that a person is who they claim to be. This is often required for activities like background checks, opening bank accounts, applying for jobs, or accessing government services.

Verifying the candidate’s personal details, such as full name, date of birth, and address.

Employment History

Verifying past employment, job titles, dates of employment, and reasons for leaving. It's a critical part of your professional profile, often required when applying for jobs, undergoing background checks, or applying for loans or visas.

To assess the candidate’s work experience and whether their past job history aligns with the resume.

Criminal Record Check

A criminal record check (also called a background check or police check) is a process of verifying whether an individual has any prior criminal convictions, charges, or other offenses recorded in their history.

To assess whether the candidate has any prior criminal behavior that could pose a risk to the organization.

Right to Work Verification

Right to Work verification is the process of confirming that an individual is legally allowed to work in a specific country.

To confirm that the candidate has the proper documentation to work without legal issues.

Reference Checks

Reference checks are a process employers use to verify information about a job candidate by contacting individuals who can provide insights into their work history, skills, performance, and character.Contacting previous employers, colleagues, or other professional references.

To gain insights into the candidate’s work ethic, skills, and overall performance in prior roles.
