Retiral Trust Fund System

Introduction to RTFS System

RTFS is a comprehensive PF Trust Management System designed to simplify and automate trust operations. It enables organizations to configure advance and withdrawal rules as per trust policies. The system automatically calculates loan eligibility and sanction amounts based on approval configurations. RTFS provides complete tracking of member applications from submission to approval. Instant automated payments are processed after online approvals. The platform offers centralized and real-time accounting for accurate financial control. It supports auto journal voucher posting with multiple approval levels.

RTFS includes powerful and customizable accounting reports. Investment and member-related reports are available for better analysis. Bulk transaction processing helps reduce operational time and effort.
